# Maintenance and Safety Practices

Operators should record unusual vibration, heat, power interruptions, and material-feed problems with the affected line, machine, and time. Notify the shift lead and maintenance team before restarting equipment after an unplanned stop.

Maintenance teams should review overdue preventive-maintenance work before returning equipment to full production. Record inspection findings, parts replaced, and the next service date.

After a supplier delay or material shortage, verify received quantities and inspect the affected lot before releasing it to production. If a quality issue is suspected, hold the lot and document inspection results.
